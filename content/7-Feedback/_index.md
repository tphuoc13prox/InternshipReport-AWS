Below are my comments and suggestions after three months in the First Cloud AI Journey program, written in the hope of helping the program improve for future cohorts.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The program puts a strong emphasis on autonomy: most of the time I studied on my own through the FCJ modules and labs and organised the work together with my project team. This requires initiative from day one, but it also got me used to finding resources, planning my own work and taking ownership of my part.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Mentors and the admin team helped whenever the team reached out with a question, for example to confirm the requirements of the report and the workshop. As a result, our team learned to prepare specific questions, with the necessary context, before asking.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
My major is Artificial Intelligence, and my main work in the project was the chatbot on Amazon Bedrock, so it was directly related to what I study. Cloud, serverless and infrastructure as code (AWS CDK) were new areas for me, and they showed me how an AI application is actually put into production.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
What taught me the most was building a project that really runs on AWS with a team of five, from the proposal to deployment and operations. In addition, AWS credits and the FCJ labs let me practise directly on real services instead of only reading documentation.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The CPAWS team worked well together: everyone had a clear role, every change went through a pull request with cross-review, and we met regularly to agree on direction. This helped us find problems early and made it clear who owned each decision.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program does not offer an internship allowance, and working hours are fixed. In return, interns get access to AWS credits, learning materials, labs and AWS community events for study and practice.

### Additional Questions

- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
  Working on a real project on AWS with my team, with enough resources (credits, labs) to practise on my own.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  
  A clearer learning path for each role in the team, and more activities that connect the intern teams with each other.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
  Yes, for friends who want to go into cloud or AI and are ready to learn on their own, because the program offers a real project but expects a high level of initiative.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?  
  Provide a suggested path of modules and labs per role (AI/GenAI, platform, data, security) when the teams are formed, so each person knows what to prioritise for their part; and organise more sharing and networking sessions between teams.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?  
  Possibly, depending on the opportunities and my plans after graduation.
