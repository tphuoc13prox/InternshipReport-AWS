# Summary Report: "Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global"

### Event Objectives

- Introduce the Buildrathon challenge, its objectives, and judging criteria.
- Provide an overview of AWS Cloud, Generative AI, and modern AI Agent development using AWS services.
- Provide insights into career opportunities and current trends in Cloud and AI.
- Encourage participants to connect with other FCAJ members and build collaboration opportunities.
- Provide practical exposure to AI Agent development through a hands-on challenge.

### Speakers

- **Thien Lu – Program Manager, FCAJ**
- **Truong Bui – Tech Lead, CMC Global**
- **Phong Pham – Program Manager, FCAJ**

### Event Information

- **Event:** Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global
- **Date:** September 26, 2026
- **Time:** 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM
- **Venue:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Organizers:** AWS First Cloud Journey (FCAJ) and CMC Global

### Key Highlights

#### Buildathon Kickoff

- Introduction to the Buildathon challenge, including its objectives and judging criteria.
- Overview of AWS Cloud and its role in developing modern applications.
- Introduction to Generative AI and modern AI Agent development using AWS services.

#### Ice-breaking and Networking

- Connected with other participants and FCAJ members.
- Shared backgrounds and interests in AWS and AI.
- Had opportunities to form connections and prepare for potential teamwork during the Buildathon.

#### Building a Career in Cloud & AI

- Learned about current trends in Cloud and AI.
- Gained insights into real-world projects and career opportunities in the technology industry.
- Learned how organizations are adopting AWS and Agentic AI to address business challenges and support innovation.

#### Q&A Session

- Participants had the opportunity to ask questions about AWS, Generative AI, career development, certifications, and the Buildathon.
- Received practical perspectives and guidance from speakers and mentors.

#### AI Agent Challenge

- Participated in a hands-on mini challenge focused on building an AI-powered solution using AWS services.
- Explored concepts including prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), knowledge bases, and AI agents.
- Applied the concepts introduced during the event in a practical development scenario with mentor support.

### Key Takeaways

#### Cloud and AI Development

- Gained a better understanding of how AWS Cloud can support the development of modern AI-powered applications.
- Learned about the role of Generative AI and AI Agents in building practical technology solutions.

#### AI Agent Development

- Gained practical exposure to important concepts in AI Agent development, including prompt engineering, RAG, and knowledge bases.
- Better understood how these components can be combined to create AI-powered applications.

#### Career Development

- Gained a broader perspective on career opportunities in Cloud and AI.
- Learned about the importance of combining technical knowledge with practical project experience.

#### Collaboration and Teamwork

- Recognized the importance of communication and collaboration when developing technology solutions.
- Connected with other participants who share interests in AWS and AI, providing opportunities for future collaboration.

### Applying to Work

- Apply AWS Cloud concepts when designing and deploying future AI projects.
- Explore the use of Generative AI and AI Agents for solving practical problems.
- Apply prompt engineering and RAG concepts when developing AI applications that require access to external knowledge.
- Consider knowledge bases as a component for improving the ability of AI applications to work with domain-specific information.
- Use the experience gained from the Buildathon to improve teamwork, communication, and project development skills in future FCAJ activities and projects.

### Event Experience

Participating in the "Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global" event provided me with both technical knowledge and practical experience in Cloud and AI.

The event helped me better understand how AWS Cloud, Generative AI, and AI Agents can be used to develop practical technology solutions. The introduction to the Buildathon also gave me a clearer understanding of the importance of transforming technical knowledge into real-world products.

The AI Agent Challenge was particularly valuable because it allowed me to apply concepts such as prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and knowledge bases in a practical scenario. This helped me connect the theoretical concepts learned through the FCAJ program with an actual AI development task.

The career discussion also provided useful perspectives on the current Cloud and AI industry, including real-world applications and career opportunities. In addition, the networking activities gave me the opportunity to interact with other FCAJ participants who share similar interests in technology.

Overall, the event strengthened my understanding of AWS and modern AI development while also highlighting the importance of practical implementation, teamwork, and continuous learning in building a career in Cloud and AI.
