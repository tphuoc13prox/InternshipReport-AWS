# Summary Report: "Smart Plushies for Children"

### Event Objectives

- Contribute to a community initiative that brings comfort and joy to children supported by Operation Smile Vietnam.
- Introduce Amazon's volunteering culture and the role of technology companies in supporting local communities.
- Provide a hands-on activity in which participants prepare smart plush toys for children using laptops and the materials provided.
- Show that technology can be used for social good, even by participants without programming or technical experience.
- Encourage interaction and teamwork between FCAJ participants and Amazon employees.

### Event Information

- **Event:** Smart Plushies for Children – Amazon Volunteering Event
- **Date:** Monday, October 26, 2026
- **Time:** 01:30 PM – 03:30 PM
- **Venue:** EC2 Room, 36th Floor, Amazon Office, Ho Chi Minh City (held at the same time at the Hanoi office)
- **Organizers:** Amazon in collaboration with Operation Smile Vietnam

### Key Highlights

#### Introduction to the Initiative

- Introduction to the "Smart Plushies for Children" initiative and its purpose.
- Overview of Operation Smile Vietnam and its work supporting children born with cleft lip and cleft palate.
- The event took place at the same time at the Amazon offices in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi.

#### Hands-on Smart Plushie Activity

- Used personal laptops together with the materials provided at the event.
- Followed step-by-step instructions from the organizers to prepare the smart plush toys.
- The activity was designed so that participants without programming or technical experience could also take part.

#### Teamwork and Community

- Worked alongside Amazon employees and other volunteers at shared tables.
- Exchanged ideas and helped one another complete the activity.

#### Feedback Session

- Shared personal experience and suggestions through the feedback form at the end of the event.

### Key Takeaways

#### Technology for Social Good

- Learned that technology can be used not only to solve business problems but also to create a positive impact on the community.

#### Corporate Social Responsibility

- Gained a better understanding of how Amazon encourages its employees to give back to the local community through volunteering.

#### User-Centered Thinking

- Recognized the importance of designing a product around the needs of its end users, in this case young children.

#### Collaboration

- Recognized that clear instructions and teamwork help people with different backgrounds complete a shared task.

### Applying to Work

- Consider the needs of end users when designing features for my FCAJ project, for example keeping chatbot explanations simple for users without a financial background.
- Write clear, step-by-step documentation so that team members with different backgrounds can follow it.
- Support teammates and share knowledge so that the team can complete tasks together.
- Look for opportunities to apply cloud and AI skills to community and social impact projects.
- Continue taking part in volunteering activities alongside technical learning.

### Event Experience

Participating in the "Smart Plushies for Children" volunteering event gave me a different perspective on technology, beyond the technical workshops of the FCAJ program.

The event showed me that technology can bring joy to people in a very direct way. Knowing that the plush toys would be given to children supported by Operation Smile Vietnam made the activity more meaningful than a regular hands-on lab.

The hands-on smart plushie activity was particularly valuable because it was designed so that anyone could join, even without technical experience. This reminded me that a good technical product should be easy to use and built around its users.

I also enjoyed working alongside Amazon employees and other FCAJ participants in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere, which made it easy to talk and help one another.

Overall, the event strengthened my understanding of Amazon's culture of giving back to the community while also highlighting the importance of empathy, teamwork, and using technology for social good.
